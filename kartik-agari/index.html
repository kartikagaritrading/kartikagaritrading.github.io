I lost money first. Then I learned why.

I'm Kartik, from Agar, a small village near Dwarahat in the hills of Almora, Uttarakhand. My childhood was not easy.

I learned trading from my brother. He is the one who motivated me to move forward.

The honest part

I mostly lost money at the start. My profits were small. Those losses became my best teacher, and they gave me four lessons:

“Trading is not gambling, and it is not a joke. Learn it seriously, and learn it with discipline.”
  • Take it seriously. Treat trading like a skill, not a game.
  • Be disciplined. Have rules, and follow them.
  • Do not trade every day. One or two trades a day is enough.
  • Do not overtrade. More trades usually means more mistakes.
