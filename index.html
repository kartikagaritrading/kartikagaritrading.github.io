Analysis on every timeframe

From 1 minute to 1 month: dozens of indicators such as RSI, MACD and moving averages combined into one meter. Change the timeframe with the tabs.

How to read the meter

Strong Sell / Sell: Most indicators point down.
Neutral: Indicators are split; the market has not picked a direction.
Buy / Strong Buy: Most indicators point up.
Combine short and long timeframes: if the 5-minute and 15-minute meters say up but the 1-day and 1-week meters say down, the bounce is often weak. The bigger timeframe carries more weight.
This is indicator maths, not a prediction. Always decide your Stop Loss before you trade.
